### 📊 Sales Performance Analysis



**Role**: Data Analyst

**Tool**: Microsoft Excel





#### **🔍 Project Overview**



This project analyzes a retail sales dataset containing transaction-level information across multiple product lines, branches, and customer demographics.



The goal was to build an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** that delivers operational and strategic insights into:



\* Revenue performance

\* Product demand

\* Payments behavior

\* Branch productivity

\* Customer segmentation





#### 📁 **Dataset Description**



The dataset consists of structured transaction records with fields such as:



\* Invoice ID

\* Branch

\* City

\* Customer Type

\* Gender

\* Product Line

\* Unit Price

\* Quantity

\* Tax (5%)

\* Total Revenue

\* Date and Time

\* Payment Method

\* Customer Rating

#### **📌 Key Analysis Performed**



* Sales by product category
* Revenue trends by month
* Branch performance comparison
* Customer demographic breakdown
* Payment method distribution





#### **📈 Dashboard Features**



The dashboard presents:



✅ Total revenue and quantity sold

✅ Sales performance by branch

✅ Product category contribution

✅ Customer gender distribution

✅ Popular payment methods

✅ Monthly sales trends



###### 

#### **💡 Key Insights**



Here are some high-level insights uncovered:



* Certain product categories consistently outperform others.
* One branch dominates overall sales volume.
* Customers mostly prefer cashless payment options.
* Revenue fluctuates by month indicating seasonality.



###### 

#### ✅ **Recommendations**



Based on the analysis:



* Increase inventory for top-performing products.
* Invest marketing resources into high-performing branches.
* Promote digital payment channels.
* Schedule promotions during low-performing periods.
